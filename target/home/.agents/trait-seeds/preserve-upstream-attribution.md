---
name: preserve-upstream-attribution
subject: Existing source attribution in a documentation rewrite or cleanup.
requirement: >
  Preserve meaningful citations and attribution to the work that informed the
  project. Do not remove or bury that context merely to shorten or polish the
  document.
applicability: >
  Applies when attribution is accurate and relevant. Correct inaccurate claims
  and obsolete references, and honor an explicit request to change their
  presentation. Source-license obligations still apply independently.
purpose: Preserve provenance and the reader's ability to assess the original work.
---

A comparison with an upstream project can explain why this project exists.
Rewording that explanation should preserve the connection and its useful links.
